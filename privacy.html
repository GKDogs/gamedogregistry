1. Who we are

The Game Dog Registry is run by Trail Keepers LLC, which operates Game Keepers ("we", "us"), in Ohio, USA. This notice explains what information the Registry collects, why, who handles it for us, and your choices. It covers the free public pedigree site and the members site at https://gkdogs.github.io/gamedogregistry/. Effective November 25, 2026.

We collect only what we need to run the Registry. We don't sell personal information, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

3. What others can see

  • Anyone, on the free public site: dog names, registered names, sex and parents, including dogs members add (marked "Added by a member"). Never who added a dog, registration numbers, photos, notes or anything about members.
  • Other members: your display name on board posts and listings, and the dogs, photos and listings you add. Not your email address.
  • Moderators and the super admin: ownership claims and proof photos, to decide them. The super admin can also see the activity log and private record notes.
  • Payments: only Stripe sees your card details.

5. Cookies, device storage and visit counts

  • No advertising or tracking cookies. The Registry doesn't use them.
  • Visit counts (free public page only): GoatCounter records which page was visited, the site that linked to it, browser and screen size, and an approximate country. It uses no cookies, and we can't identify you from it. The members site isn't counted.
  • Storage on your device (members site): to work, the members site saves a few things in your browser: your sign-in session, the time you were last active (so idle sessions end), your recent searches, a copy of the registry's dog records so pages load quickly, and the dog finder if you've used it. Registration numbers are never saved on your device. Signing out ends your session; clearing your browser's site data removes the rest.
  • Photo dog finder: when you frame a photo, the dog finder runs in your own browser.

6. Keeping it, your choices and the rest

  • How long we keep it. Your account information stays while you have an account. After you ask us to delete it, we remove it within 30 days. Pedigree records you added (names and parents) stay on the Registry, because other dogs' pedigrees depend on them (Membership Terms, section 5); we no longer link them to you. Board posts, listings, claims and photos are deleted with your account. The activity log is kept for up to 2 years. Stripe keeps payment records as financial law requires.
  • Backups. We keep private backup copies of the database to recover from mistakes; deleted information leaves them as older backups are replaced.
  • Your choices. You can see and change your display name and the dogs you added on the members site. Email gamekeepersdogs@gmail.com to get a copy of your information, correct it, or delete your account. You can cancel your membership any time from Account → Manage billing.
  • Security. Access to member information is limited by database rules, sessions time out, passwords are never stored readably, and payments go only through Stripe. No system is perfectly secure; tell us if you think your account was misused.
  • Children. The Registry is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger; if we learn we have, we delete it.
  • Changes. We'll post any update here with a new effective date, and email members about significant changes before they take effect.
  • Contact. Trail Keepers LLC (Game Keepers), Ohio, USA · gamekeepersdogs@gmail.com
