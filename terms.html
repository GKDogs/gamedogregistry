3. Membership and payment

  • Price. Membership is $25.00 per year (USD), plus any tax that applies. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
  • Automatic renewal. Your membership renews automatically each year at the then-current price until you cancel. If the price changes, we'll tell you by email at least 30 days before your next renewal.
  • Cancelling. Cancel any time from Account → Manage billing. You keep member access until the end of the year you've paid for; it doesn't renew after that.
  • Refunds. We don't refund part of a membership year. If you joined for the first time in the last 7 days and haven't used the members site, ask at gamekeepersdogs@gmail.com and we may refund you at our discretion.

  • What lapses. When a membership ends, member-only features stop, and your stud and store listings are hidden from other members until you rejoin. Dogs you added stay on the Registry (section 5).

4. The Registry's records

  • Independent. The Registry is run by Trail Keepers LLC (Game Keepers). It is not the ADBA, UKC or any other registry, and is not endorsed by them. It does not register dogs or issue papers.
  • Accuracy. Records come from registry pedigrees, other published pedigrees and members. We work to keep them right, but some will be incomplete or wrong. "Pedigree-confirmed" means we checked that dog's parents against a registry pedigree; other records have not been checked. Always check an official registry before you rely on a record.
  • Figures are estimates. COI, blood percentages and pairing results are calculated from the records on file and are only as complete as those records. They are information, not breeding, veterinary or purchasing advice.
  • Corrections. If a record is wrong, tell us at gamekeepersdogs@gmail.com or through the members site. We decide what the record shows; where sources disagree, the ADBA pedigree wins.
  • What's public. Dog names, sex and parents appear on the free public site. Registration numbers, owners' details and private notes do not.

5. What you add

Members can add dogs, photos, board posts, stud and store listings, and ownership claims ("your content").

  • Be accurate and honest. Only add dogs you have good reason to believe are real, with the parents you believe are correct. Only claim dogs you actually own, and send true proof.
  • Keep it clean. No fight or match records in names or notes, no owners' home addresses or phone numbers, and nothing that identifies a private person without their consent.
  • Photos. Only upload photos you took or have permission to use.
  • Dogs you add go public right away. A dog's name, registered name, sex and parents appear on the free public site as soon as you save it, marked "Added by a member" until we check it. You can edit or delete dogs you added; photos and registration numbers stay members-only.
  • Permission to use it. You keep ownership of your content. You give us a free, worldwide, non-exclusive licence to store, display, copy and adapt it to run the Registry, including keeping pedigree records (names and parents) after you delete your account, because other dogs' pedigrees depend on them.
  • Moderation. We may edit, hide or remove any content, confirm or reject any claim, and mark records as checked or unchecked, at our discretion.
  • Limits. Daily limits apply to posts, listings, claims and photo uploads to prevent abuse.
